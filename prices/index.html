Insurance

Registered with major private insurers

Our clinicians work with Aviva, Bupa, Cigna, Healix, Simplyhealth, Vitality and WPA. Share your policy details when booking so we can confirm cover before your visit.

Click on your insurance provider logo for more info

Before attending the centre for any treatment or service you intend to claim through your insurer, please contact your insurance provider in advance. They may require you to complete specific paperwork to bring to your appointment. It's also important to check any policy limits or excesses that may apply. You must confirm that your treatment is covered and obtain prior authorisation from your insurer before booking your appointment.
