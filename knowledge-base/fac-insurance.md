# Foot & Ankle Centre Insurance Guidance

Sources: `prices/index.html` and all files under `insurance/` in the supplied website.

## General insurance rule

The pricing page lists Aviva, Bupa, Cigna, Healix, Simplyhealth, Vitality and WPA. Before attending for any service intended to be claimed through insurance, patients are told to contact their insurer in advance, check policy limits/excesses and any paperwork/referral requirements, confirm that treatment is covered, and obtain prior authorisation where required. The assistant must not guarantee policy cover.

## AVIVA insured patients

Source: `insurance/aviva-insured-patients.html`

AVIVA health insurance members can use their policy to cover consultations and treatment at the Foot & Ankle Centre. Here is what to do before your visit.

### What you need to know

- Call AVIVA on the number printed on your membership card to request pre-authorisation for your appointment.
- Ask for written confirmation or a pre-authorisation code that includes the procedure name or consultation details.
- Email the authorisation to Email reception so we can update your records before you arrive.
- Bring your AVIVA membership card and photo ID to your appointment.
- While The Foot and Ankle Centre is happy to accept self-referrals, your insurance provider may require a GP/Consultant referral prior to attending.
- Contact AVIVA if you have any doubt regarding your policy coverage prior to attending. The Foot and Ankle Centre cannot speak to your insurance company on your behalf.
- In all cases through AVIVA, The Foot and Ankle Centre will bill your insurance company for each consultation. It is your responsibility to inform AVIVA prior to each consultation.
- If AVIVA fail to cover your consultation fees, The Foot and Ankle Centre will send you an invoice in the post to be paid within 30 days.
### Things not covered

- Unfortunately, AVIVA do not cover dressings, medication or medical devices such as orthotics and splints. Your podiatrist can discuss if self-pay fees apply.
### Obtaining an authorisation code

- Please ensure that you gain a pre-authorisation number from your insurance company and understand how much your policy excess should be by calling AVIVA on 0800 068 3827 .
- Find out your policy excess.
- Below we have given the provider numbers of our podiatrists who are registered with AVIVA, as well as the procedure codes that you will need to give them to obtain an authorisation code.
- We strongly recommend that you request all of our podiatrists be included on your authorisation code.
### AVIVA procedure codes for podiatrists

- AVIVA initial consultation - No code required
- AVIVA follow up consultation - No code required
- AVIVA gait analysis and biomechanical evaluation - No code required
- AVIVA nail surgery - S7010

## BUPA insured patients

Source: `insurance/bupa-insured-patients.html`

BUPA patients are welcome at the Foot & Ankle Centre. Follow the steps below so that we can invoice BUPA directly for your care.

### What you need to know

- It is your responsibility to inform BUPA and gain pre-authorisation prior to each consultation. The Foot & Ankle Centre cannot speak to BUPA on your behalf. If you fail to gain pre-authorisation, you will be liable for any costs incurred at the company’s self-pay fee rate.
- If BUPA fail to cover your consultation fees, The Foot & Ankle Centre will send you an invoice via email, payable within 14 days.
- While The Foot & Ankle Centre can accept self-referrals, your insurance provider may require a GP or consultant referral before you attend. Contact BUPA on 03456 090 111 or use live chat at bupa.co.uk .
- The Foot & Ankle Centre will bill BUPA UK directly on your behalf; any policy excess must be paid to the clinic.
- The Foot & Ankle Centre charges a £30 self-pay fee following each consultation to cover administrative costs, dressings or medications used. This fee covers the shortfall not included within BUPA’s contractual arrangements.
- BUPA agree consultation fees with individual podiatrists, not with The Foot & Ankle Centre. Patients are advised of the additional company fees and liability when booking.
- The Foot & Ankle Centre will bill your insurance company for each consultation on behalf of the podiatrist.
- If you have an excess payment, BUPA will contact you when it is due; the excess must be paid directly to The Foot & Ankle Centre.
- Contact BUPA if you have any doubt regarding your policy coverage prior to attending.
### Things not covered

- Medical devices such as orthotics, splints and medications are not covered by BUPA.
- BUPA will fund a gait analysis appointment when you have pre-authorisation for procedure codes AA641 and AA365; no additional clinic fees apply.
- BUPA do not fund podiatrists to administer shockwave therapy. This remains a self-pay service.
- BUPA do not fund podiatrists to use diagnostic ultrasound. This remains a self-pay service.
- BUPA do not fund podiatrists to administer cortico-steroid injection therapy or hyaluronic acid. These remain self-pay services.
- BUPA will fund a podiatric surgeon to undertake injection therapy following referral from the podiatrist.
- BUPA do not pay for routine foot care or other conservative management of foot conditions. These services can be booked separately as self-pay appointments.
### Obtaining an authorisation code from BUPA to fund your appointments

- Prior to attending, ensure that you gain a pre-authorisation number for each appointment.
- Ensure that all of our registered podiatrists are named on your policy.
- Find out your policy excess.
- Please find below a list of BUPA recognised podiatrists and their individual provider numbers.
- Contact the admin team at The Foot and Ankle Centre with your authorisation code and member number to book your procedure.
### BUPA provider numbers

- Foot and Ankle Centre - 60012594
- Mr Joseph Olivelle - 85001603
- Mr Stuart Metcalfe - 85001372
- Mr Michael Hutchby - 85013384
### Helpful resources

- Visit the BUPA member portal to check benefits and download policy documents.
- Need a tailored report for BUPA? Email Email reception with your policy number and we will send it securely.

## Healix insured patients

Source: `insurance/healix-insured-patients.html`

Healix members can use their policy for eligible podiatry care at the Foot & Ankle Centre. Please arrange approval with Healix before attending.

### What you need to know

- Contact Healix before your appointment to request funding for your podiatry care.
- Healix does not usually require treatment codes, but ISC codes are listed below in case they are requested.
- Ask Healix for a claim reference and letter of guarantee. The letter should include the authorisation code we need when billing Healix for your care.
- Email your policy number, claim reference and authorisation code to Email reception before your appointment.
- Bring your Healix membership details and photo ID to your appointment.
- While The Foot and Ankle Centre can accept self-referrals, your insurance provider may require a GP or consultant referral before you attend.
- Contact Healix directly if you have any doubt about your policy coverage, restrictions, excess or benefit limits.
### Things not covered

- Healix may fund podiatry treatments and orthotic devices where required, but cover depends on your individual policy.
- If Healix sets a fixed funding amount and your care exceeds that allowance, you will need to self-fund the remaining balance.
- If Healix does not cover any consultation, treatment, device or excess charge, The Foot & Ankle Centre will invoice you directly.
### Obtaining an authorisation code

- Contact Healix before booking or attending and request approval for podiatry care at The Foot & Ankle Centre.
- Ask Healix to confirm your policy excess, any treatment restrictions and whether orthotics or medical devices are included.
- Give Healix the relevant ISC code below if they request a code for your consultation or treatment.
- Send your policy number and authorisation code to the clinic so we can bill Healix on your behalf.
### Healix procedure codes for podiatrists

- Initial consultation - SP000005
- Follow-up consultation - SP000004
- Advanced MSK analysis - SP000259
- SWIFT - SP000259
- Shockwave therapy for plantar fasciitis - SP000259
- Shockwave therapy for Achilles - SP000259
- Injections - SP000259
- Laser therapy - SP000259
- Nail surgery - SP000259
