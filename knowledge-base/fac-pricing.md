# Foot & Ankle Centre Pricing

Source: `prices/index.html` in the supplied website files.

Use only these published prices. `POA` means a personalised quotation is required after assessment/consultation. Do not turn a procedure price into a different consultation price.

## Requests for a surgery quotation

Treat this as a surgery quotation enquiry when the patient is asking what an operation, procedure, correction or treatment might cost, even if they do not use the word “surgery”. This includes wording such as “Can I get a quote if I send photos?”, “How much would it cost to separate my toes?”, or “my second and third toes are partially joined by skin”.

Descriptions such as joined toes, toes joined by skin, webbed toes, fused toes and syndactyly may relate to **Webbed Toe Separation**. Do not diagnose or confirm suitability from a message or photographs; the clinical team must assess the condition and treatment plan.

For a quotation enquiry, explain that the team can provide an initial quotation based on clear photographs. Ask the patient to use the website enquiry form or contact **enquiries@footandanklecentre.com** with photographs of their feet and the type of surgery they are considering. The clinical team will review the images and confirm the quotation or next steps. Surgical fees are personalised and shown as **POA** where applicable.

## Surgery package note

Foot & ankle and minor surgery package pricing is tailored to the case and includes local anaesthetic, sterile dressings and two follow-up checks. Patients should request a consultation to receive an exact quotation.

If a service is not listed on the current pricing page, reception should provide a bespoke estimate rather than the assistant inventing a fee.
