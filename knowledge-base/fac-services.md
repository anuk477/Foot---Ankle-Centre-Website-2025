# Foot & Ankle Centre Services

Sources: `index.html`, `booking.html`, `assistant/knowledge.json`, `prices/index.html`, and procedure pages in the supplied website.

This file lists the services and procedures presented by Foot & Ankle Centre. The procedure list below mirrors the website's **Procedures** dropdown so the assistant can reliably recognise every named service patients may ask about.

## Procedure directory

### Foot & Ankle Surgery

- **Scar-free Toe Shortening** — Surgical procedure offered by Foot & Ankle Centre. A consultation is required to assess suitability.
- **Corn Removal Surgery** — Surgical treatment option for appropriate corn problems. A clinician must determine whether surgery is appropriate.
- **Bunion Correction** — Surgical bunion correction offered by Foot & Ankle Centre. Suitability is determined at consultation.
- **Webbed Toe Separation** — Surgical procedure for webbed toes, including toes that a patient describes as partially joined by skin or soft tissue. Assessment is required before treatment is recommended; the fee is personalised/POA.
- **Hammertoe Correction** — Surgical correction for hammertoe deformity. Suitability is assessed by the clinician.
- **Lumps and Bumps Removal** — Removal of appropriate foot lumps and bumps following clinical assessment.
- **Ingrowing Toenail Removal** — Surgical treatment for appropriate ingrowing toenail cases following assessment.
- **Fix My Flat Foot** — Foot and ankle surgical service relating to flat-foot correction. The assistant must not assume surgery is required; recommend assessment first.

### Chiropody

- **Hard Skin Removal** — Chiropody treatment for hard skin/callus concerns.
- **Verruca Treatment** — Assessment and treatment options for verrucae.
- **Corn Removal** — Chiropody treatment for corns.
- **Long Nails** — Routine nail care for long or difficult-to-manage toenails.
- **Ingrowing Toenails** — Assessment and treatment for ingrowing toenail concerns. This is distinct from the surgical **Ingrowing Toenail Removal** procedure.
- **Fungal Nails** — Assessment and treatment guidance for suspected fungal nail concerns. The assistant must not diagnose a fungal infection without clinical assessment.

### Podiatry

- **Foot Pain** — Podiatry assessment for foot pain and related concerns.
- **Plantar Fasciitis** — Assessment and treatment options for plantar fascia/heel-pain presentations. The assistant must not diagnose plantar fasciitis solely from symptoms.
- **Insoles** — Assessment and provision of insoles/orthotic solutions where clinically appropriate.
- **Cushions for Feet** — Cushioning/padding solutions for appropriate foot problems following assessment.
- **Foot Botox** — Foot Botox service offered by the clinic. Suitability and indications must be determined by a clinician.

### Physiotherapy

- **Shockwave Therapy** — Shockwave treatment used where clinically appropriate as part of a treatment plan.
- **Myofascial Release** — Hands-on treatment aimed at myofascial restriction and related musculoskeletal symptoms where appropriate.
- **Triggerpoint Therapy** — Treatment targeting clinically identified trigger points where appropriate.
- **Exercise Rehabilitation** — Exercise-based rehabilitation prescribed according to assessment and recovery goals.
- **Electrotherapy** — Physiotherapy modality used where clinically appropriate.
- **Dry Needling** — Needling technique used by an appropriately trained clinician where suitable.
- **Cupping Therapy** — Cupping treatment offered as part of appropriate physiotherapy/musculoskeletal care.
- **Acupuncture** — Acupuncture treatment offered where clinically appropriate.
- **Sports Massage** — Sports massage for appropriate musculoskeletal and activity-related concerns.

### Musculoskeletal Health

- **Ultrasound Guided Injections** — Injection procedures performed with ultrasound guidance where clinically indicated. Assessment is required first.
- **Biomechanics** — Assessment of foot and lower-limb mechanics, posture and related musculoskeletal concerns.
- **Gait Analysis** — Assessment of walking/running gait where appropriate.
- **X-ray Facilities** — X-ray facilities available as part of appropriate clinical assessment and investigation.

## Appointment categories available on the booking form

### Foot & Ankle Surgery
Request a consultation to discuss surgical options. Choosing this category does not mean the patient needs surgery.

Published procedures include scar-free toe shortening, corn removal surgery, bunion correction, webbed toe separation, hammertoe correction, lumps and bumps removal, ingrowing toenail removal and Fix My Flat Foot.

### Cosmetic Foot Surgery
Request a consultation about a cosmetic foot concern. Suitability and treatment are decided by the clinician.

The homepage groups cosmetic/surgical services with procedures including lumps and bumps, bunions, hammertoes, cosmetic foot surgery and related corrective procedures.

### Podiatry Appointment
For a foot or nail concern when a podiatry assessment is appropriate. The website lists Foot Pain, Plantar Fasciitis, Insoles, Cushions for Feet and Foot Botox under Podiatry.

### Chiropody Appointment (Routine Care)
Routine nail and skin care. Website procedures include Hard Skin Removal, Verruca Treatment, Corn Removal, Long Nails, Ingrowing Toenails and Fungal Nails.

The homepage describes routine care as nail care, removal of hard skin, corns, cracked heels, ingrown toenails, plus home-care and footwear advice.

### Musculoskeletal Health
For assessment of foot or ankle pain, walking mechanics, gait and related musculoskeletal concerns. Website procedures include Ultrasound Guided Injections, Biomechanics, Gait Analysis and X-ray Facilities.

The wider website also describes musculoskeletal concerns such as heel pain, forefoot pain, knee pain, posterior tibial tendinopathy, shin splints, flat feet and high-arched feet.

### Physiotherapy
For physiotherapy assessment, rehabilitation or an existing physiotherapy plan. Website procedures include Shockwave Therapy, Myofascial Release, Triggerpoint Therapy, Exercise Rehabilitation, Electrotherapy, Dry Needling, Cupping Therapy, Acupuncture and Sports Massage.

### Choosing a category
This option is available on the booking form when the patient does not know which category is appropriate. Reception confirms the appropriate appointment.

## Published clinic-wide service summary

The website states that Foot & Ankle Centre provides podiatry, chiropody, physiotherapy and foot and ankle surgery for patients across Chingford, Wanstead, Westcliff-on-Sea, London and Essex. It describes assessment of foot pain, ankle pain, skin and nail concerns, sports injuries, gait problems and long-term conditions, with treatment recommendations based on the patient's activity level and recovery goals.

## Important assistant rules

- Do not infer that a patient needs a procedure merely because their symptoms resemble a condition named on the website.
- Use the service categories above as starting points and leave diagnosis and treatment decisions to the clinician.
- Where a procedure normally requires assessment first, make that clear to the patient.
- Do not invent prices. Use `fac-pricing.md` for published prices and direct the patient to reception when a price is not listed.
- If the correct appointment category is unclear, use **Choosing a category** or direct the patient to reception.
