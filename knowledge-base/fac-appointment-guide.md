# Foot & Ankle Centre Appointment Guide

This guide is derived from the booking categories linked by the clinic's procedure pages and from `booking.html`. It is for appointment routing only, not diagnosis or clinical triage.

If the patient's concern is unclear, complex, or does not map cleanly to one of the categories below, choose **reception guidance** or ask reception to help rather than guessing.

## Chiropody Appointment (Routine Care)

Procedure pages currently routed to this booking category:
- **Corn Removal** — Safe and effective removal of corns to relieve pressure and pain, with advice to minimise recurrence.
- **Fungal Nails** — Assessment and treatment options for fungal toenails, with practical guidance to improve appearance and prevent spread.
- **Hard Skin Removal** — Comfort-focused care to safely remove callus and hard skin, improve comfort, and reduce pressure points.
- **Ingrowing Toenails** — Gentle, effective care to relieve pain from ingrowing toenails and help prevent recurrence. We offer conservative treatment and clear advice on home care.
- **Long Nails** — Professional trimming and thinning for long or thickened, bruised or distorted toenails to restore comfort and reduce risk of problems.
- **Verruca Treatment** — Evidence-based verruca care with tailored treatment plans and prevention advice.

## Podiatry Appointment

Procedure pages currently routed to this booking category:
- **Cushions for Feet** — Soft padding and cushions reduce pressure on sore areas, helping protect skin and relieve discomfort.
- **Foot Botox** — Botulinum toxin (Botox) may be used for excessive sweating (hyperhidrosis) or select muscle-related discomfort in the feet.
- **Foot Pain** — Foot pain can come from joints, tendons, nerves, skin pressure, footwear, injury or the way your foot moves. We assess the cause and build a treatment plan around your symptoms and goals.
- **Insoles** — Supportive insoles can improve alignment and comfort for a range of foot and lower-limb conditions. We advise on custom and prefabricated options.
- **Plantar Fasciitis** — Plantar fasciitis is a common cause of heel and arch pain, often worse with the first steps in the morning or after rest. We assess the drivers and tailor treatment to reduce pain and improve load tolerance.

## Musculoskeletal Health

Procedure pages currently routed to this booking category:
- **Biomechanics** — In-depth biomechanical assessment to understand how your feet and lower limbs move during walking and activity. Helps diagnose causes of pain and informs tailored treatment plans.
- **Diagnostic Injections** — Diagnostic injections help identify the exact source of pain and guide the most effective treatment.
- **Gait Analysis** — We assess walking and running mechanics to identify patterns that may contribute to pain or injury and to guide effective treatment.
- **Ultrasound Guided Injections** — Ultrasound guidance improves accuracy and comfort when delivering injections for inflammatory and painful conditions.
- **X-ray Facilities** — We can arrange access to X-ray facilities where appropriate to support rapid diagnosis and treatment planning.

## Physiotherapy

Procedure pages currently routed to this booking category:
- **Acupuncture** — Used alongside physiotherapy and podiatry treatments, acupuncture may help reduce pain and support recovery.
- **Cupping Therapy** — Negative pressure therapy to help relieve muscle tension, promote circulation, and support recovery.
- **Dry Needling** — Very fine needles target trigger points to reduce muscle tension and pain. Often paired with rehab.
- **Electrotherapy** — Therapeutic electrical modalities to relieve pain and support tissue repair, used as part of a wider rehab plan.
- **Exercise Rehabilitation** — Progressive exercise plans designed to restore strength, control and confidence after injury or surgery.
- **Myofascial Release** — Hands-on therapy targeting tight fascia and trigger points to restore mobility and reduce pain.
- **Shockwave Therapy** — Non-invasive treatment to stimulate healing for plantar fasciitis, Achilles tendinopathy and other conditions.
- **Sports Massage** — Targeted soft tissue treatment to ease muscle tightness, support recovery and improve comfortable movement.
- **Triggerpoint Therapy** — Targeted pressure techniques to release muscle knots, reduce pain, and improve flexibility.

## Foot & Ankle Surgery

Procedure pages currently routed to this booking category:
- **Bunion Correction** — Modern bunion correction tailored to your foot and goals. Our team will advise on surgical and non-surgical options.
- **Corn Removal Surgery** — Permanent removal of painful corns when conservative care isn't enough. Performed under local anaesthetic for quick recovery.
- **Fix My Flat Foot** — Our flat foot correction plans combine advanced gait analysis, bespoke orthoses and minimally invasive surgery where appropriate. We tailor every approach to reduce pain, improve arch support and restore confident movement.
- **Hammertoe Correction** — Straightening and stabilisation of hammertoes to relieve pain and improve function, with a focus on safe recovery.
- **Ingrowing Toenail Removal** — Relief from painful ingrowing toenails with partial nail avulsion and targeted aftercare to reduce recurrence.
- **Lumps & Bumps Removal** — Targeted removal of painful ganglions, cysts and soft-tissue bumps so you can walk comfortably and fit back into your shoes without pressure.
- **Scar-free Toe Shortening** — Shortens one or more toes to improve comfort and footwear fit. Performed by experienced podiatric surgeons with a focus on safe recovery and great outcomes.
- **Webbed Toe Separation** — Cosmetic and functional separation of congenitally webbed toes by an experienced team with careful aftercare.

## Quick routing examples from published site content

- Hard skin/callus, corns, long nails, fungal nail concerns, verrucae or routine ingrowing-toenail discomfort: start with **Chiropody Appointment (Routine Care)**.
- Foot pain or plantar-fascia/heel-pain assessment listed under Podiatry: start with **Podiatry Appointment** unless the relevant procedure page routes differently.
- Gait, biomechanics, movement/load issues, orthotic planning, or musculoskeletal foot/ankle concerns: start with **Musculoskeletal Health**.
- Rehabilitation, soft-tissue treatment or physiotherapy-plan questions: start with **Physiotherapy**.
- A patient specifically asking to discuss a listed surgical correction: use **Foot & Ankle Surgery** or the exact category linked by that procedure page. This is a consultation request and does not mean surgery is required.
- Cosmetic-foot concerns: use **Cosmetic Foot Surgery** when that is the patient's stated goal; suitability is decided clinically.

## Booking limitation

The website booking form accepts preferred date and time only. Submitting the form does **not** book or reserve an appointment. Reception contacts the patient to confirm the date and time.
